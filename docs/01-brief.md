# Salon Queue — Brief

## Problem
On weekends, customers wait 1–2 hours at the salon without knowing when their turn is. Some leave without being served, and others argue about who arrived first. Staff have no single place to see the real queue.

## Users
- **Customer**: joins and tracks the queue from a phone.
- **Receptionist**: manages the queue on a tablet and adds walk-ins.
- **Barber**: sees their current and next customer on a phone.
- **Owner**: manages services, barbers on duty, and opening/closing the queue.

## Must-have features
1. Join the queue from a phone: name, phone number, 1–3 services, barber or "Any barber".
2. Live customer status: token, position, estimated wait, "Start coming" alert, "It's your turn" alert.
3. Receptionist dashboard: add walk-ins, call next per barber, mark no-show, cancel.
4. Barber screen: current customer, Start / Done, On break / Available toggle.
5. Waiting-area TV: who is with which barber, plus the next 5 tokens.
6. Owner settings: services (time, price, on/off), barbers on duty, open/close online joining, today's stats.
7. English and Gujarati labels with a language toggle.
8. Staff screens protected by a simple PIN.

## Don't build
Online payment, future-date appointments, SMS/WhatsApp notifications, loyalty or discounts, multiple branches, user accounts or passwords for customers.

## Look & feel
Premium salon feel: dark charcoal + gold accent, rounded cards, clean typography. Mobile-first for customer and barber screens; tablet-landscape for reception; large-text TV view readable from 5 metres.
